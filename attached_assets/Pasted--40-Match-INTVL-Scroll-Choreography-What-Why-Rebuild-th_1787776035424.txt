#40 - Match INTVL Scroll Choreography
What & Why
Rebuild the HexRunner marketing website’s scroll presentation so its pacing, stacked full-screen cards, pinned scenes, layered transitions, and content swaps closely match the live INTVL reference. The reference is interaction inspiration only: all shipped branding, copy, imagery, video, and product UI remain original HexRunner material.

Done looks like
A fresh desktop browser audit records the live reference from hero to footer, including intermediate scroll states and approximate viewport-length timing
The page feels like one continuous rounded, outlined card deck rather than unrelated sections
Hero, handset, world-stage, gameplay, activity, feature, FAQ, and closing CTA transitions visibly hold, overlap, and hand off at reference-like moments
The gameplay phone has four coordinated product states, including leaderboard, while the corresponding numbered step becomes visually active
Feature cards use active-card layering and meaningful fan/stack movement over HexRunner video footage
Existing prototype download modal behavior remains accessible and unchanged
Desktop, tablet, short-phone, and reduced-motion experiences remain complete, readable, and free of clipped or blank scroll ranges
Full-page browser verification confirms no broken HexRunner assets, uncaught errors, or scroll-animation startup failures
Out of scope
Copying INTVL trademarks, wording, source code, font files, images, videos, or other proprietary media
Changes to the Expo mobile app, API server, gameplay backend, or database
Publishing the website
Steps
Audit the live reference -- Scroll the current INTVL homepage from top to footer in a fresh desktop browser and capture representative intermediate states, timings, sticky boundaries, and layer behavior.
Build the shared card deck -- Replace isolated section stacking with a continuous full-viewport card framework that preserves rounded outlines, overlap, and clear scene-to-scene handoffs.
Choreograph the opening scenes -- Tune hero hold, rising handset, and world-stage sequences with reference-like pin durations, independent depth layers, media scaling, and staged metrics.
Rebuild the product walkthrough -- Coordinate four phone states and active numbered steps across a long pinned runway, using HexRunner videos/posters and smooth cross-fade/translation transitions.
Rebuild conversion scenes -- Give the activity split, feature card fan, FAQ handoff, and lime finale the same persistent, layered scroll behavior and active-card depth ordering.
Adapt accessibility and responsive motion -- Preserve the detailed desktop choreography while providing deliberate tablet/mobile sequencing and a complete reduced-motion path without clipping or empty scroll space.
Verify the full journey -- Run production checks and browser tests across desktop, short mobile, keyboard/modal interactions, reduced motion, and the complete top-to-footer scroll.
